# Project Rules & Strategic Guidelines

## Goalkeeper Selection Strategy

When selecting, recommending, or optimizing goalkeepers for Fantasy Premier League squads, follow strictly one of two tactical strategies. Never select two expensive premium goalkeepers (e.g., two £5.0m+ keepers without a planned rotation schedule):

---

### Strategy 1: "Set-and-Forget" Premium Goalkeeper
- **Composition**: Exactly **one premium starting goalkeeper** (e.g., David Raya at £6.0m, Gianluigi Donnarumma at £5.5m) paired with a **£4.0m backup goalkeeper** (e.g., Forster, Steele, Matthews, Bentley).
- **Tactical Role**:
  - The premium goalkeeper plays **every single gameweek**, regardless of fixture difficulty.
  - The £4.0m backup keeper is effectively a **deadspot / bench fodder** whose sole purpose is to minimize wasted budget so funds can be maximized across the starting XI outfield players.
  - Total goalkeeper budget: **£9.5m – £10.0m**.
  - No rotation tracking is necessary since the premium goalkeeper is locked in every match.

---

### Strategy 2: Rotating Budget Goalkeepers (Pairing Strategy)
- **Composition**: Two playing starting goalkeepers:
  - At least one goalkeeper must be priced at **£4.5m**.
  - The maximum allowable price point for either keeper is **£5.0m** (acceptable pairings: £4.5m + £4.5m, or £5.0m + £4.5m).
  - Total goalkeeper budget: **£9.0m – £9.5m**.
- **MANDATORY INSTRUCTION**:
  - Whenever this strategy is used, **you MUST provide a gameweek-by-gameweek rotation schedule** stating explicitly which goalkeeper starts in which gameweek based on fixture difficulty (FDR) and clean sheet probabilities.
  - *Example Format*:
    - **GW1**: Keeper A (Home vs Opponent, FDR 2) — *Keeper B on bench (Away vs Top 6)*
    - **GW2**: Keeper B (Home vs Opponent, FDR 2) — *Keeper A on bench*
    - **GW3**: Keeper A ...
